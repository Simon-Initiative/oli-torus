### User Need

As an instructor, I want contextual explanations of student-facing features and special cases in student preview, so that I can understand how students will experience the course and recognize any instructor-only controls.

### Acceptance Criteria

**Successful behavior**

- Student preview displays an **Instructor preview note** wherever additional context is needed to explain student-facing behavior or instructor-only controls.
- Each note is clearly labeled **Instructor preview** note and appears next to the content or control it explains.
- Notes explain the relevant behavior and distinguish what students experience from what is available only to instructors.
- Initial use cases include:
  - **Activity bank selection questions:** Explain how the question shown in preview relates to the selection students receive and identify any instructor-only controls.
  - **Gating conditions:** Explain the conditions that control student access and clarify how those conditions affect the preview.

**Exceptions and safeguards**

- Instructor preview notes and associated instructor-only controls are never displayed to students.
- Notes appear only where relevant and do not replace or obscure student-facing content.
- Explanatory notes do not receive keyboard focus **unless** they contain an interactive element.

### Accessibility Requirements

- **Structure and labels:** Include the visible text Instructor preview note and programmatically associate it with the note content.
- **Reading order:** Place each note in a logical reading order next to the content it explains.
- **Keyboard and focus:** Any actions within a note must be keyboard operable, have descriptive accessible names, and show visible focus. Follow a logical focus order without moving focus automatically to static notes.
- **Assistive technology:** Static notes must be available through normal screen-reader navigation without alert announcements. Student-facing views must exclude instructor preview notes and controls from both the visible interface and the accessibility tree.

## Technical Guidance

### Delivery requirements

Show the Instructor preview note beside bank-selected questions in authorized Student Preview.

- **Scope:**
  - Explain that the question is an example of what students may receive; preserve normal question interaction and saved selections.
  - Keep management/customization controls in their applicable instructor surface, rather than copying them into Student Preview.
  - Actual learners and separate author previews receive no note.
- **Technical approach:** Reuse the existing source-selection provenance in delivery activity references and the shared renderer. Pass server-derived preview eligibility through LiveView/controller rendering contexts. Proposed implementation details are provisional; required behavior and binding constraints remain mandatory.
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  - **Positive:**
    - AC1: In authorized instructor/admin Student Preview, each relevant bank-selected question has a nearby programmatically labeled Instructor preview note explaining how the example relates to questions students may receive. Any instructor-only action shown in its applicable perspective is clearly identified.
    - AC2: The note follows reading order, with descriptive links and visible focus.
  - **Negative:**
    - AC4: No note appears on unrelated content, in actual learner markup/accessibility trees, or in separate author-preview contexts. Ordinary delivery URLs alone do not hide the note from authorized Student Preview.
    - AC5: The note does not take focus or announce as an alert.
    - AC6: Student Preview keeps normal question interaction and gains no Manage questions or customization controls from the instructor wrapper.
- **Size:** M.
- **Feature flags:** None.

### Implementation guidance

- **Scope analysis:**
  - Add the informational note beside realized bank questions in authorized instructor/admin Student Preview on ordinary delivery routes. Preserve normal question interaction and persisted selections.
  - Preserve the existing Instructor View sample presentation and clearly identify its management actions as instructor-only. Do not copy those controls into Student Preview.
- **Sources and design:** [Design Handoff](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XrwiN86AETdRxxyoGanQE2pAvl484RHPM6w0prWKk6c/edit); [bank-selection note](https://www.figma.com/design/S8qAFhjjlnRyTHCg2Dyb8p/User-Preview-Overhaul?node-id=529-11932); [Instructor Interface](https://www.figma.com/design/S8qAFhjjlnRyTHCg2Dyb8p/User-Preview-Overhaul?node-id=484-14126).
- **Analysis:**
  - **Design evidence:** Frame `529:11932` shows the active Student preview perspective and an Activity bank selection / Instructor preview note above an interactive question. The handoff's Instructor preview notes section explicitly requires these notes in Student Preview. The label identifies the audience, not the active perspective. The frame shows no Manage questions control in Student Preview.
  - **Existing behavior:** `ActivityProvider.replace_with_reference/2` in `lib/oli/delivery/activity_provider.ex` adds `source-selection` to realized activity references. `test/oli/delivery/activity_provider_test.exs` covers this provenance and multiple selections. `Student.Utils.build_html/3` renders persisted attempt content through `Oli.Rendering.Context`; `Oli.Rendering.Activity.Html.activity/2` receives the activity reference. `PageDeliveryController` also builds delivery rendering contexts. The existing instructor React card shows a sample and management/customization controls.
  - **Remaining gap:** Student delivery rendering does not use the selection marker to show the authorized preview note. Restricting the change to `ActivityBankSelectionPreview.tsx` would miss the designed Student Preview surface and risk bringing management controls into it.
  - **Verification:** Development must verify note placement for multi-question selections, eligibility across LiveView/controller paths, exclusion from actual learner and separate author-preview contexts.
- **Technical analysis:**
  - **Binding constraints:** Include the note in authorized Student Preview while excluding actual learners and separate author-preview contexts. Preserve ordinary delivery interaction, persisted selections, and Instructor View's management permissions. The informational note does not add management controls to Student Preview.
  - **Proposed rendering:** Use existing `source-selection` metadata in delivery activity references to place the labeled note beside realized bank questions. Supply a narrow, server-derived eligibility value to rendering contexts from [T-002](https://eliterate.atlassian.net/browse/MER-6223)'s actual-actor and delivery-context handling, covering LiveView and controller entry. Ordinary delivery mode or `preview_mode: false` alone does not establish eligibility. Reuse the normal activity renderer and interaction bridge; no new selection engine, provenance store, or migration is needed.
  - **Existing instructor controls:** Preserve the existing Instructor View card and actions. Reuse explanatory wording where relevant without restricting the note to the Instructor View React wrapper. Bank-management header suppression, local back navigation, and return focus belong to [T-003](https://eliterate.atlassian.net/browse/MER-6224), within the prerequisite Instructor view to student preview Story.
