## Description

## Scope

Create/reuse the project’s marked preview section and keep its full publication hierarchy current through Publish-triggered jobs.

## Acceptance criteria

### Positive

- First authorized creation uses the latest publication; repeated/concurrent creation reuses one current section.
- Every successful Publish schedules the required update for an existing preview section even with ordinary-section auto-push disabled; rolled-back publishes leave no update job.
- Updates apply the full publication/hierarchy coherently, refresh affected caches, and preserve existing attempts/progress, including retained active-attempt revisions.
- New preview sections have a durable marker queryable independently of their current association.

### Negative

- Concurrent first creation/Publish cannot lose an update; obsolete jobs cannot regress the section or act on a replacement association.
- Failed updates do not expose a partial completed state. Optional ordinary-section pushes cannot create competing outdated preview updates.
- No draft content, per-author section allocation, automatic progress reset, cleanup job, or marking of ordinary/product sections is introduced.

## Technical Guidance

Reuse compatible section creation, update processing, external-tool initialization, and cache invalidation. Enforce one current project association and coordinate first creation with Publish. Enqueue updates transactionally from successful publishing, independently of optional pushes to ordinary sections. The update strategy must apply additions/removals and hierarchy changes rather than inherit blueprint minor-only update behavior. MER-6236 owns launch readiness/LiveView; MER-6237 owns failed-state replacement.

Use the parent [F-007](https://eliterate.atlassian.net/browse/MER-6217) for shared feature requirements, design sources, and code evidence. This sub-task is complete when its scoped acceptance criteria above and the applicable shared constraints are satisfied.

### Readiness must survive job pruning

Keep the section's applied-publication state consistent with completion of its structural update. A queued, scheduled, executing, or retryable job counts as an update in progress only when it targets the current managed preview section and can bring it to the required publication. Obsolete jobs for a retired section or an older publication must not keep the current preview in Updating indefinitely.

Torus enables `Oban.Plugins.Pruner` in `config/config.exs` and `config/runtime.exs`; completed, cancelled, and discarded jobs are not permanent readiness records. The launch check must still recognize a complete, current section after its successful job is pruned, and a stale section with no remaining applicable live job must be recoverable. Reuse publication and job state where sufficient; do not require permanent job retention or a new monitoring service.

- **Acceptance:** After a successful update and pruning of its completed job, the current, complete preview is still ready.
- **Acceptance:** Failed, cancelled, or pruned job history cannot leave a stale preview permanently Updating. Coordinate the state read with existing Publish/update/reset transaction boundaries so it does not mistake an in-flight transition for an abandoned update.
