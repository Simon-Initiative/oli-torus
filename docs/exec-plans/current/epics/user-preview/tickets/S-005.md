# S-005: Explain bank examples and simplify selection management

- **Status:** Created and verified
- **Qualification:** Qualified. The design and written handoff require bank-selection notes in authorized Student Preview. Delivery already retains selection provenance, but does not render the note. Existing Instructor View management and local back navigation can be reused.
- **Lane:** A, fifth.
- **Ticket:** [MER-6186](https://eliterate.atlassian.net/browse/MER-6186)

## Description

Show the Instructor preview note beside bank-selected questions in authorized Student Preview, and simplify the selection-management header.

- **Requirements:** [MER-6158](https://eliterate.atlassian.net/browse/MER-6158), R-002, R-001, R-008. Full requirements are in the epic ticket.
- **Scope:**
  - Explain that the question is an example of what students may receive; preserve normal question interaction and saved selections.
  - Keep management/customization controls in their applicable instructor surface, rather than copying them into Student Preview.
  - Hide the global header and selector in selection management, retain local back navigation, and restore the containing page and opener focus on return. Actual learners and separate author previews receive no note.
- **Technical approach:** Reuse the existing source-selection provenance in delivery activity references and the shared renderer. Pass server-derived preview eligibility through LiveView/controller rendering contexts, then adapt the existing management header and return behavior. Proposed implementation details are provisional; required behavior and binding constraints remain mandatory.
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  - **Positive:**
    - AC1: In authorized instructor/admin Student Preview, each relevant bank-selected question has a nearby programmatically labeled Instructor preview note explaining how the example relates to questions students may receive. Any instructor-only action shown in its applicable perspective is clearly identified.
    - AC2: The note follows reading order, with descriptive links and visible focus.
    - AC3: Management keeps local back navigation; returning restores the containing page header/selector and opener focus or a logical fallback.
  - **Negative:**
    - AC4: No note appears on unrelated content, in actual learner markup/accessibility trees, or in separate author-preview contexts. Ordinary delivery URLs alone do not hide the note from authorized Student Preview.
    - AC5: The note does not take focus or announce as an alert.
    - AC6: Management shows neither instructor header nor selector; existing bank actions/filter behavior remain intact. Student Preview keeps normal question interaction and gains no Manage questions or customization controls from the instructor wrapper.
- **Prerequisites:** S-002, S-003.
- **Size:** M.
- **Feature flags:** None.

  - Add the informational note beside realized bank questions in authorized instructor/admin Student Preview on ordinary delivery routes. Preserve normal question interaction and persisted selections.
  - Preserve the existing Instructor View sample presentation and clearly identify its management actions as instructor-only. Do not copy those controls into Student Preview.
  - Remove the global instructor header/selector from selection management; retain local back navigation and restore opener focus. Preserve selection, customization actions, and filters.
- **Sources and design:** [Design Handoff](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XrwiN86AETdRxxyoGanQE2pAvl484RHPM6w0prWKk6c/edit); [bank-selection note](https://www.figma.com/design/S8qAFhjjlnRyTHCg2Dyb8p/User-Preview-Overhaul?node-id=529-11932); [Instructor Interface](https://www.figma.com/design/S8qAFhjjlnRyTHCg2Dyb8p/User-Preview-Overhaul?node-id=484-14126).
- **Analysis:**
  - **Design evidence:** Frame `529:11932` shows the active Student preview perspective and an Activity bank selection / Instructor preview note above an interactive question. The handoff's Instructor preview notes section explicitly requires these notes in Student Preview. The label identifies the audience, not the active perspective. The frame shows no Manage questions control in Student Preview.
  - **Existing behavior:** `ActivityProvider.replace_with_reference/2` in `lib/oli/delivery/activity_provider.ex` adds `source-selection` to realized activity references. `test/oli/delivery/activity_provider_test.exs` covers this provenance and multiple selections. `Student.Utils.build_html/3` renders persisted attempt content through `Oli.Rendering.Context`; `Oli.Rendering.Activity.Html.activity/2` receives the activity reference. `PageDeliveryController` also builds delivery rendering contexts. The existing instructor React card shows a sample and management/customization controls, while `BankSelectionManagerLive` already has a local back path.
  - **Remaining gap:** Student delivery rendering does not use the selection marker to show the authorized preview note. Restricting the change to `ActivityBankSelectionPreview.tsx` would miss the designed Student Preview surface and risk bringing management controls into it. Management still includes the excluded global header, and opener focus restoration remains unverified.
  - **Verification and uncertainty:** Targeted subagent code/test inspection confirms existing provenance and shared rendering paths; no tests or browser flows were run. Development must verify note placement for multi-question selections, eligibility across LiveView/controller paths, exclusion from actual learner and separate author-preview contexts, and return focus.
- **Technical analysis:**
  - **Binding constraints:** Include the note in authorized Student Preview while excluding actual learners and separate author-preview contexts. Preserve ordinary delivery interaction, persisted selections, and Instructor View's management permissions. The informational note does not add management controls to Student Preview.
  - **Proposed rendering:** Use existing `source-selection` metadata in delivery activity references to place the labeled note beside realized bank questions. Supply a narrow, server-derived eligibility value to rendering contexts from S-002's actual-actor and delivery-context handling, covering LiveView and controller entry. Ordinary delivery mode or `preview_mode: false` alone does not establish eligibility. Reuse the normal activity renderer and interaction bridge; no new selection engine, provenance store, or migration is needed.
  - **Management and return:** Reuse the existing Instructor View card and actions. Remove the global header from selection management while retaining its local back target and navigation context. Restore focus to the opener or a logical fallback on return. Reuse the explanatory wording where relevant without restricting the note to the Instructor View React wrapper.
- **Prerequisite handoffs:** S-002 for authorized actor/context eligibility in Student Preview rendering; S-003 for complete selector/focus-return acceptance. S-004 is a scheduling predecessor only.
- **Sizing analysis:** M; reuse existing selection provenance, shared rendering, and local back navigation. Main uncertainties are context eligibility, multi-question note placement, and browser focus restoration.
