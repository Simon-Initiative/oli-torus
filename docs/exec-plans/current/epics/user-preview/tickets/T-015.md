## Description

Provide the authorized Full Course Preview launch LiveView used by Review and Publish. Open it in a new tab and automatically navigate to Learn in that tab when the managed section is ready.

## Technical Guidance

Authorize project access and reject never-published projects before resolving/creating the managed section. Use MER-6235's association/update state; launch checks do not rebuild or refresh an existing section. Supply the validated launch context to MER-6241's identity integration and expose MER-6237's reset action.

Classify readiness from consistent section/publication/job state:

- **Ready:** The latest publication and structural update are complete, even if the completed job was pruned. Unrelated old failures do not block launch.
- **Updating:** Applicable queued, scheduled, executing, or retryable work can still bring the current managed section up to date.
- **Failed:** The section is stale/incomplete and no applicable live work remains, including after cancellation, exhausted retries, or terminal-job pruning. Offer Reset Preview; do not require a retained failed-job record.

Show “Preview is updating. You will be taken to the course when it is ready.” while waiting. There is no Check Again button. Check on mount/reconnect and after reset, and automatically detect later state changes. Periodic checks while connected, optionally accelerated by existing broadcasts, are a suitable simple approach; broadcasts alone are insufficient. Avoid overlapping checks and stop when the view exits/redirects. Exact timing and wiring are engineering decisions.

On failure, show the reason, Reset Preview with its shared-progress explanation, and Exit Preview. Successful reset rechecks readiness and enters Learn when ready; failed reset retains recovery. Recovery initiated elsewhere is also detected. Exit closes the tab, assuming success, without return tracking or fallback redirect.

Keep the actual author and real learner login unchanged. Do not reuse hidden-user login/token substitution in `ProductsController.preview_launch/2` or `UserAuth.auto_enroll_admin/2`. Preview links/requests carry separately validated author/current-preview-user/section context; exact transport is provisional. Revalidate authorization and association before redirecting.

### Size and feasibility

- **Size:** L.
- **Single-engineer feasibility:** Feasible once section/update state is defined. The UI is modest; readiness classification, pruning, reconnects, automatic navigation, and recovery transitions drive effort.

## Acceptance criteria

- Both entry points use this LiveView; ready previews enter Learn immediately and updating previews redirect automatically when ready, in the same tab. No extra tabs or update jobs are created by checks.
- Cover completion before mount, missed notifications, reconnect, stale/obsolete jobs, pruning, and concurrent Publish/update/reset. Never show partial or stale course content or offer reset because of an inconsistent read.
- Announce changed status without moving focus or repeating unchanged announcements. Reset/exit remain keyboard usable; expose busy state and prevent duplicate reset submissions.
- Temporary check failure shows connection/check feedback and retries automatically; it does not invent a permanent failure or readiness result. Revoked access prevents navigation and further preview use.
- A real learner tab and multiple project previews retain their own identities/context through interaction, refresh, reconnect, and tab close. Authors without a learner login gain no browser-wide learner login; invalid/reset contexts never fall back to another user.
- Preserve saved progress and product/template behavior. Initial creation failure prevents launch and presents an actionable error.
